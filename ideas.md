# Richard Cook Strategic Communications — Design Direction

## Ground-truth brief

The user-supplied framework and copy are authoritative. The site is a seven-page portfolio and advisory profile for Richard Cook Strategic Communications, with the primary navigation: Home; What Richard Cook Brings; Areas of Expertise; Working with Richard; Integrating Strategy, Media and Reputation; Richard’s Work — Case Studies; Testimonials.

## Chosen approach: Burnt Orange Briefing

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial modernism with the discipline of a print briefing document: clean, classic, typographic, and quietly authoritative.

### Core Principles

1. **Clarity before decoration.** Large, confident headlines and simple hierarchy let the supplied copy carry the experience.
2. **Senior and discreet.** The site should feel like an adviser’s trusted briefing, not a loud agency pitch.
3. **Evidence over claims.** Case studies preserve dates, geographies, scope, confidentiality notes, and outcomes supplied by Richard.
4. **Deliberate restraint.** Burnt orange is used as a clear signal against white and near-black, never as a decorative gradient.

### Color Philosophy
Burnt orange is the signature signal: warm, credible, and memorable without feeling corporate-blue. White provides breathing room for long-form reading. Near-black is reserved for body text and deep anchor panels where high contrast matters. Reversed text is used only on solid burnt-orange or black backgrounds.

### Layout Paradigm
A responsive editorial briefing: wide asymmetric desktop reading columns with a narrow vertical index rail, and a compact mobile header. Long-form pages use numbered sections, thin rules, pull quotes, and a generous measure. Case studies behave like report chapters rather than cards in a generic grid.

### Signature Elements
A burnt-orange vertical index line; section labels set in small uppercase mono; oversized numerals and compact metadata; orange underline animations that reveal the interaction rather than adding ornament.

### Interaction Philosophy
Navigation is always available and the current page is explicit. Calls to action are direct and discreet. Links have clear hover and focus states. The site does not pretend that a contact form is functional until a destination is supplied.

### Animation
Use only transform and opacity with a brisk editorial easing. Page content rises slightly into place on load; list rows reveal sequentially; links draw a burnt-orange underline from left to right. Keep interactions under 300ms and respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use Helvetica Neue where available, with Arial as a resilient fallback. Headlines are heavy, tight, and oversized. Body text is black, relaxed, and readable. Metadata is uppercase with generous letter spacing. The type system intentionally resembles a contemporary corporate-affairs briefing rather than a design portfolio.

### Brand Essence
**Richard Cook helps leaders and organisations make sense of complexity, communicate with authority and protect credibility when the stakes are high.**

Personality: **credible, discreet, incisive**.

### Brand Voice
Concise, clear, senior, and specific. Headlines state the challenge or capability. CTAs invite a confidential conversation without sales language. Example lines: “Clear, credible strategic communications, media counsel and crisis response.” “Facing a communications, reputation or media challenge? Arrange a confidential conversation with Richard.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a simple geometric north-star/registration mark beside the wordmark “RICHARD COOK / STRATEGIC COMMUNICATIONS.” Keep the symbol text-free, bold, and visible at header scale. It is a brand cue, not a decorative illustration.

### Signature Brand Color
Burnt Orange — `#C9512B`.

## Copy integrity

Use the supplied copy as authoritative, editing only for layout, punctuation, and responsive readability. Preserve confidentiality language. Do not add client names, ratings, testimonials, or outcomes beyond the attachment. Contact email and LinkedIn URL were not supplied, so the site uses a visible content-ready contact CTA without pretending to submit data.

## Assets

The generated geometric mark can be reused as a small header symbol if it remains visually compatible. Generated atmospheric imagery should remain secondary to the supplied copy; no image should be presented as a real client project or documentary record.

## End state

Every route should feel like part of one considered briefing document, with persistent navigation, strong mobile behavior, and a clear route to the confidential-conversation prompt.

## Style Decisions

The original Studio North placeholder direction is superseded by this Richard Cook-specific brief. The final site is intentionally text-led and should not use generic agency filler, invented social proof, or fake form submission states.
